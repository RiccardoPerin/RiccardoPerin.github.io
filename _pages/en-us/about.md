I'm Riccardo, a Bioengineering MSc student at the **University of Padova**, working where **machine learning meets medicine**. What motivates me are models that clinicians can actually trust: accurate, but also interpretable, grounded in physiology and honest about their uncertainty.

After a **BSc in Biomedical Engineering (110/110)**, I've applied ML to clinical problems wherever I could: predicting NICU admission from neonatal data, classifying brain tumours from MRI and now detecting meals from continuous glucose monitoring as part of the [DIANA project](/projects/diana/), a digital twin for pediatric Type 1 Diabetes. In **February 2027** I'm moving to Stockholm for my Erasmus+ Master's thesis at **KTH**, on [explainable AI for cancer risk assessment](/projects/kth-thesis/).

Throughout my studies I've **always worked**, in different roles, to be financially independent: as a **tutor for programming in Python**, and as the developer of a [compliance management system](/projects/scadenziario/) that a construction company now uses every day. Balancing exams, research and real users has taught me to **adapt quickly** to new contexts, to organise my time, and to take responsibility for work that other people depend on.

**Research interests**

- Interpretable machine learning on clinical and omics data
- Hybrid mechanistic–ML models: digital twins, physiological and pharmacometric (PK/PD) modelling, neural ODEs
- Computational approaches to cancer risk and early detection
- Deep learning for biomedical signals and images

I'm **looking for a PhD position starting autumn 2027**, ideally in a group that combines machine learning with mechanistic or clinical modelling. Email is the best way to reach me.
