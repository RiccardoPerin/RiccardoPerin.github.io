Things I build for fun, usually to try out a new tool.

**F1 Fantasy predictor.** A simulation-based model for choosing the team in a fantasy Formula 1 championship.

**PokerStats.** A web app in vanilla HTML/CSS/JS with Firebase, used to track poker sessions among friends. It has a leaderboard and a composite score: 70% normalised net result, 30% normalised return.

**Gym tracker.** A minimal Flutter workout tracker, with no login required.
