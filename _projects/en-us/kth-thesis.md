### Topic

My Master's thesis will develop **interpretable machine-learning models that estimate cancer risk from environmental exposure factors**. The focus is on explanations that are meaningful both at population level (which exposures drive risk overall) and for an individual (why a specific person's estimated risk is high or low).
The thesis is part of a Digital Futures research project at KTH that is building an open-source, explainable toolkit to help clinicians assess personalised breast and prostate cancer risk from genetic, environmental and epigenetic factors, using Swedish national health registries.
